    Map key

    • C1Cart - estimated position, in the cart's colour
    • JKStaff member - estimated position (initials)
    • Accuracy circle - they are most likely inside it; smaller is better
    • Dashed circle - low confidence: few beacons heard there
    • Ring - true position (simulation only)
    • Ceiling beacon - B01, B02 and so on
    • Rack beacon - small beacon on each rack (zoom in close); bright when the selected person hears it
    • Lines - beacons the selected person hears
